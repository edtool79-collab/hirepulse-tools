What it does

TikTok Product Research & Video Growth Radar

A TikTok-focused product research and video growth radar that ranks product topics by measured growth on the same video IDs across different collection times. It analyzes time-separated view-count deltas and added views, normalizes daily growth rates, and combines momentum, creator-account breadth, engagement ratios, and persistence into a ranked research-priority score with classification, rationale and next-action guidance. The Actor returns per-product research reports that cite supporting videos, per-video comparison windows, source URLs and blockers, measurements of added views and normalized growth, creator-account breadth and concentration metrics, repeat-growth evidence, and agent-friendly decision fields describing missing evidence and suggested checks. The system supports importing historical exports or using an optional capped cloud upstream scraper for live collection, establishes baselines and watch cohorts for cohort research, enforces minimum comparison windows between measurements, and applies explicit scoring heuristics and alert thresholds to identify candidate emerging products.

Use cases

  • Rank product topics for further research by measured video-level growth over time.
  • Detect emerging product trends on TikTok by identifying videos with significant added views and high normalized daily growth.
  • Distinguish broad-based trends from single-creator spikes through creator-account breadth and concentration analysis.
  • Produce per-product research reports that include supporting videos, growth metrics, persistence checks, and decision rationale for human or agent review.
  • Monitor and refresh an existing video cohort (baseline and watch reports) to detect repeat growth without replacing the cohort.
  • Import existing historical exports to establish baselines and compare later observations without initiating new upstream collection.
  • Evaluate ranking performance with top-k labeling and pairwise precision measurement using later-observed outcomes.
  • Provide machine-readable decision outputs for agents, including research-priority scores, classifications, missing evidence, and suggested next checks (relevance, sourcing, landed cost, fulfilment).
