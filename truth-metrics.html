Why this page is boring on purpose

The growth plan this replaced proposed simulating activity: bots calling the gateway, fabricated usage history and invented customer stories. None of that is here. Two reasons. First, owner runs are excluded from Apify's public counters, so simulated volume cannot move the number a buyer looks at. Second, a buyer who discovers a padded metric stops being a buyer. What is published here is smaller and verifiable.
