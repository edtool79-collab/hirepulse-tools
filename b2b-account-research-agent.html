What it does

B2B Account Research & Enrichment

A domain-to-signal enrichment Actor that converts company websites into structured account intelligence: it extracts live open-job counts from supported applicant-tracking systems, hiring pace and role breakdowns, technologies detected on the website and named in job postings, sales and GTM signals from the home page, DNS-derived email setup, publicly published contacts, and site localization. The Actor uses job boards' public APIs and attribution rules (link evidence or domain evidence) to produce job counts and posting dates; it parses HTML (including Google Tag Manager) for analytics/ad pixels, CMS, frameworks, e-commerce, payments and CDN signals; it queries public DNS for mail-provider, SPF and DMARC information; and it extracts emails, phone numbers and social profiles published on company pages. Outputs are provided as one JSON record per company with separate section-level status and source evidence, and include quoted job-text evidence for technologies named in postings and attribution metadata for ATS-derived counts.

Use cases

  • Prioritizing accounts by open-job counts, hiring pace, department-level hiring signals and source evidence for sales or outreach sequencing.
  • CRM enrichment with company-level hiring signals, web technology stack, sales-motion indicators (pricing/signup/demo/contact signals) and public contact details.
  • Monitoring or recurring account checks to detect new hiring, newly mentioned technologies in job postings, or changes to website/compliance signals.
  • Tech-stack discovery and ad/analytics pixel inventory for vendor targeting or competitive research.
  • Building automated sales and research agents, workflow integrations, and enrichment steps in automation tools and AI agents.
