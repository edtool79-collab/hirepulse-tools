What it does

Ad-Waste & AI Conversion Leak Auditor

An e‑commerce audit Actor that scans lists of store domains to detect conversion and ad-spend leakage by using cookieless HTTP probes and platform fingerprinting; it identifies storefront types (Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento, BigCommerce, Next.js), flags missing remarketing/analytics pixels and recovery widgets (examples: email marketing tags, Meta Pixel/CAPI, TikTok Pixel, GA4/GTM, live chat/cart-recovery), evaluates checkout friction and conversion funnel degradation into a numeric severity score, verifies AI shopping-agent discoverability and structured data (checks for /llms.txt manifests, robots.txt rules for search bots, ChatGPT/Perplexity agent visibility, and Product/Offer JSON‑LD markup), and generates concise, domain-specific cold outreach hooks that cite the exact missing pixel or AI schema issue.

Use cases

  • Audit large lead lists of e‑commerce stores to prioritize prospects by conversion leakage and ad-waste severity.
  • Detect missing remarketing, analytics, and live‑chat/cart recovery implementations across storefront platforms.
  • Perform GEO-aware AI shopping‑agent readiness checks (ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, /llms.txt, robots.txt, Product/Offer JSON‑LD).
  • Quantify funnel loss with a severity score to triage remediation efforts or paid outreach.
  • Automatically produce hyper-specific two‑sentence outreach pitches citing exact missing pixels or schema errors.
