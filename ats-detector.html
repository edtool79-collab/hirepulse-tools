What it does

ATS Detector

A domain-scanning tool that detects a company’s applicant tracking system (ATS) and locates its public careers board, returning board metadata such as detected ATS vendor (examples: Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, Recruitee, SmartRecruiters), the public board URL, and available job count. Operates by analyzing company websites and supported ATS board patterns using public and platform-native sources, producing one metadata record per domain suitable for recruiting automation, B2B data enrichment, and job board discovery workflows.

Use cases

  • Find which ATS a company uses and locate its public careers board.
  • Prepare recruiting workflows using detected ATS and board metadata.
  • Enrich B2B company records with careers-board information and open-job counts.
  • Batch-process company domains to generate one board-metadata record per domain.
