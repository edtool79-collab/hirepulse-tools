What it does

YouTube Transcript Scraper

A YouTube-focused transcript extraction Actor that retrieves available captions and subtitle text from videos (including Shorts and live replays), optionally returning segmented timestamps and language information alongside video metadata; it performs bulk/batch processing to produce structured transcript records and metadata useful for retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), research, content workflows, automation, and AI agent ingestion. Core keywords: YouTube captions, transcript extraction, subtitles, timestamps, language tags, batch processing, metadata (title, channel, duration, URL, video identifier), structured transcript records, content analysis, RAG ingestion.

Use cases

  • Ingest YouTube video captions for retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and vector indexing.
  • Collect transcripts at scale for video research and content analysis.
  • Export structured transcript records for content workflows and automation platforms.
  • Provide timestamped caption segments and language metadata for AI agents and downstream processing.
