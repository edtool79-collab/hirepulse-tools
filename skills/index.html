AGENT SKILLS

Skills for your coding agent

One SKILL.md file per tool. Each says when to use the tool, what it returns, what it does not return and how it is priced. Copy the file into your agent's skills folder.

aliexpress-product-price-search

Search AliExpress products by keyword with custom currency and ship-to country to extract prices, original prices, discount percentages, star ratings, units sold, sponsored flags, listing dates, images, and product URLs (hirepulse/aliexpress-search-scraper). Use for ecommerce price comparison, dropshipping product research, finding best-selling AliExpress items, or exporting AliExpress search results to JSON/CSV/Excel.

b2b-company-buying-intent

Enrich B2B company domains with live buying-intent signals, active hiring counts, Google Ads activity, organic SEO traffic, MX email provider, and a 0-100 intent score (hirepulse/company-intent-radar). Use when qualifying sales leads, building AI SDR workflows, enriching domains for Clay or CRM pipelines, or checking if a company is actively growing and spending.

domain-organic-traffic-seo

google-ads-competitor-spy

Spy on competitor Google Ads by domain using the Google Ads Transparency Center API (hirepulse/google-ads-transparency-scraper). Use when the user asks to see what Google ads a company or competitor is running, check active ad creatives (text, image, video), verify advertisers leading to a domain for brand protection, monitor new competitor ads, or get a domain ad summary without needing Google Advertiser IDs.

google-jobs-salary-search

Search Google Jobs across 90+ countries by keyword, city, and radius to extract job listings, parsed min/max salaries, contract types, posting dates, and direct apply links (hirepulse/google-jobs-scraper). Use when searching jobs by role or location across multiple employers, benchmarking salaries, or replacing SerpApi Google Jobs API.

google-keyword-volume-cpc

Get Google monthly search volume, CPC (USD), competition, keyword difficulty, search intent, top-of-page bid ranges, monthly/quarterly/yearly trends, and keyword ideas in bulk (hirepulse/keyword-search-volume). Use when doing SEO keyword research, evaluating PPC ad bids, finding keyword ideas from a seed keyword, or checking optional AI search volume.

greenhouse-lever-ashby-jobs

Scrape open jobs, published salary ranges, departments, locations, and detected tech stacks from company ATS job boards including Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, Recruitee, and SmartRecruiters (hirepulse/ats-job-pulse). Use when extracting all open roles at specific target companies by domain or board slug, analyzing a company's tech stack from job descriptions, or tracking new and closed job postings.

youtube-transcript-timestamps

Extract transcripts and captions from YouTube videos and Shorts in bulk with exact start/duration timestamps, full plain text, word counts, channel metadata, and language detection (hirepulse/youtube-transcript-scraper). Use when summarizing YouTube videos, extracting spoken quotes with timestamps, indexing video content for RAG, or converting YouTube captions to JSON/text.
