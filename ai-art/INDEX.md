# Hirepulse AI Art Asset Index

Comprehensive catalog of generated abstract 3D brand and tool visuals for the **hirepulse** dark-theme developer/data platform (pay-per-use data APIs for AI agents).

## Design System & Theme
- **Background**: Deep obsidian near-black (`#0a0a10`)
- **Primary Accent**: Luminous violet (`#b4a0ff`)
- **Secondary Accent**: Electric cyan / teal highlights (`#38bdf8` / `#00f5d4`)
- **Style**: Octane 3D photorealistic render, cinematic dark volumetric lighting, raytraced glass and caustics, shallow depth of field with macro bokeh.
- **Constraints**: 100% abstract and metaphorical. Zero text, numbers, brand trademarks, logos, UI screenshots, or human faces.
