What it does

Google Jobs Alternative: ATS & Public Job Listings Search

Searches public ATS boards and job feeds to discover and extract structured job listings for recruiting, talent sourcing, and job‑market research. Operates in dual modes: company‑domain discovery that finds postings on supported public ATS boards, and keyword search across public feeds (including Arbeitnow, Remotive, Jobicy). Produces structured job records containing job titles, employer and location data, salary details when available, source identifiers, and application links, with optional job descriptions and store categorization. Query routing and geographic filtering are source‑dependent (some sources rank matches rather than enforcing strict geographic filters); single‑word queries are treated as keyword searches while company domains trigger ATS discovery.

Use cases

  • Recruiting and candidate sourcing from public ATS boards and job feeds
  • Talent sourcing using keyword searches across multiple public job feeds
  • Public job‑market research and trend analysis based on structured job records
  • Discovering employer postings via company domain (ATS discovery)
