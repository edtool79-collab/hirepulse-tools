What it does

ATS Job Scraper (hirepulse/ats-job-pulse)

A scraper that discovers open jobs and hiring signals for a company domain by crawling company careers pages and supported applicant tracking systems (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, Recruitee, SmartRecruiters). It extracts structured job records and compact company hiring summaries containing job titles, locations, salary information when available, application links, role counts, remote/onsite indicators, and hiring-summary metrics. The Actor can filter roles, aggregate per-company hiring data, and perform repeated-run comparisons to detect new, removed, or changed postings by establishing a baseline. Sources include public and platform-native ATS boards with optional external data providers; outputs are structured JSON-style job and company summary records suitable for downstream analysis and monitoring.

Use cases

  • Recruiting and talent sourcing: discover current openings and candidate-facing details across multiple ATS platforms.
  • Hiring monitoring and signal detection: detect new or changed job postings and track hiring activity over repeated runs.
  • B2B account research and intent: surface hiring signals and open roles at target companies for sales or account research.
  • Job data extraction and aggregation: produce structured job records and compact company summaries for market intelligence or headcount analysis.
  • ATS-specific collection: extract openings from Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, Recruitee, and SmartRecruiters.
