What it does

Company Intent Radar

An Actor that qualifies companies for B2B outreach by combining hiring signals, advertising evidence, website technology fingerprints and email/DNS infrastructure checks into a single account report. It aggregates ATS job listings, Google Ads transparency records and website-technology observations, inspects ad creatives and ad pixels for destination attribution, and probes email infrastructure (DNS/MX) and HTTPS reachability. The Actor produces a compact, structured account report with an explainable intent estimate (score with an evidence breakdown), hiring indicators and status, detected applicant-tracking system, counts of open and recent job postings, advertising presence and attribution evidence, site-technology findings, and email infrastructure observations.

Use cases

  • Qualify a company for B2B outreach with an evidence-backed intent estimate
  • B2B lead enrichment using hiring, ads and website signals
  • Account qualification for sales or marketing prioritization
  • Competitor research by combining hiring and advertising signals
  • Inspect public email infrastructure and source availability for outreach planning
