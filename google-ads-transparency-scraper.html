What it does

Google Ads Transparency Scraper

A domain-focused scraper that queries Google Ads Transparency Center records to extract advertiser and creative records tied to specified website domains. It retrieves structured creative metadata and advertiser details including creative formats (image, video, text), creative assets and identifiers, observed date ranges (first/last seen and run-duration), domain attribution evidence linking creatives to destination domains, country and platform filtering options, periodic monitoring for repeated runs, and store/category labeling for advertisers. Keywords: Google Ads Transparency Center, advertiser records, creative metadata, domain attribution, creative formats, image creatives, video creatives, text creatives, observed dates, advertiser identity, country/platform filters, monitoring.

Use cases

  • Competitor ad research to discover ads associated with a specific domain.
  • Creative analysis and extraction of image, video and text ad assets and metadata.
  • Brand monitoring by tracking advertiser activity over time via first/last seen dates and run duration.
  • Domain-level attribution of creatives based on destination-domain evidence.
  • Filtering and analysis by country and platform, and repeated-run monitoring for changes.
  • Assigning store/category labels to advertiser records for categorization and comparison.
