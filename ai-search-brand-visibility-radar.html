What it does

AI Crawler Readiness Audit & llms.txt Draft

A website audit Actor that inspects a public domain to produce a structured AI-crawler readiness report, an action/prioritized change list, and an optional draft llms.txt generated from observed site information. The Actor analyzes robots.txt directives (producing guidance for named crawler tokens including GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended and CCBot), fetches homepage response headers, discovers llms.txt observations and a fuller llms-full view, extracts JSON-LD schema types and identity/link signals, compiles local readiness scores with supporting HTTP evidence, and emits a concise executive summary plus an optional llms.txt draft for review. Outputs are structured audit findings that combine HTTP/robots/metadata evidence, schema and identity link extraction, score-based readiness indicators, and prioritized remediation recommendations.

Use cases

  • Audit a public website for readiness to be crawled by AI-focused crawlers and bots.
  • Generate an editable llms.txt draft based on observed site information for review before publication.
  • Evaluate robots.txt coverage and produce per-crawler guidance for major AI crawler tokens (GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, CCBot).
  • Extract and report JSON-LD schema types and site identity/link signals used by AI systems.
  • Produce local readiness scores with HTTP evidence to prioritize site changes and remediation tasks.
  • Compare a primary domain against selected competitor or category signals to inform site preparation for AI indexing.
