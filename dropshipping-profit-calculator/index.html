THE MATH / NOTHING HIDDEN

Every cost has a place.

Percentage fee = selling price × your fee percentage ÷ 100

Profit = selling price − product cost − shipping − percentage fee − fixed fees − ad cost − other costs

Margin (%) = profit ÷ selling price × 100

Break-even ad cost = selling price − product cost − shipping − percentage fee − fixed fees − other costs

The browser calculates locally. Inputs stay on this page and are not saved or sent to an API.
