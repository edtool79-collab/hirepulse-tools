What it does

Company Contact Finder: Emails, Phones, Socials & DNS

Extracts publicly listed business contact information from company domains by crawling company websites and querying public DNS records; collects email addresses, phone numbers, contact-page links, and social-profile URLs, and can optionally inspect email infrastructure signals such as MX, SPF, DMARC and email-provider indicators. Produces structured contact records per domain containing contact points and email-infrastructure observations useful for lead enrichment, B2B contact research, sales intelligence, outreach and company website analysis.

Use cases

  • B2B contact research and lead enrichment
  • Company website research and contact discovery
  • Social-profile discovery for companies (LinkedIn, Twitter/X, etc.)
  • Email infrastructure inspection and assessment (MX, SPF, DMARC, provider signals)
