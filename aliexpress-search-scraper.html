What it does

AliExpress Search Scraper

A web-scraping Actor that searches AliExpress and extracts structured product search-result metadata for price comparison, ecommerce research, and sales monitoring. It performs keyword-based search queries on AliExpress, supports sorting and regional options (currency and ship-to country), and returns per-result product metadata such as titles, pricing (current and original), discount information, ratings and review counts, product identifiers, product links, images, and search-position information as structured records for downstream analysis.

Use cases

  • Price comparison across AliExpress listings for procurement or repricing workflows
  • Ecommerce product research and market analysis (catalog discovery, price ranges, discounts)
  • Monitoring product listings and sales signals (ratings, review counts, discount trends)
  • Competitive intelligence and cross-border pricing analysis using currency and ship-to-country settings
  • Bulk extraction of search-result metadata for inventory mapping or catalog enrichment
