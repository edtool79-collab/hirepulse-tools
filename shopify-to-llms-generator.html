What it does

Store Catalog to llms.txt Draft

Converts sampled public Shopify and WooCommerce product feeds into llms.txt draft artifacts for owner review. The Actor crawls product listings, samples up to 100 products from accessible feeds, extracts product metadata (titles, descriptions, observed pricing, images, variants and related catalog facts), and generates both concise and full plain-text llms.txt drafts. Each run produces a catalog draft plus metadata about the crawl (product counts, crawl status and HTTP evidence) and preserves diagnostics for review; the catalog draft is presented as a draft (not claimed to be a UCP-conformant profile).

Use cases

  • Generate llms.txt draft files from public Shopify or WooCommerce product feeds for owner review and editing.
  • Produce a sampled catalog draft for spot-checking or reviewing portions of a store inventory.
  • Collect crawl diagnostics and HTTP evidence to validate feed accessibility and fetch results.
  • Create both concise and full plain-text drafts suitable for manual curation, content review, or downstream content generation.
