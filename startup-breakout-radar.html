What it does

Startup Breakout & Growth Radar

A domain-level research Actor that detects startup growth signals by scanning public hiring boards, Google Ads Transparency, website technology footprints and published business contacts, then aggregates structured company profiles with a 0–100 breakout score, categorical signal tier and plain-language reasons tied to source evidence. Technical capabilities include parallel section scraping of public ATS listings (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, SmartRecruiters, Recruitee) to derive hiring-intent metrics (open roles, recent dated postings, role functions), sampling Google Ads Transparency creatives and identifiers to detect advertising activity, passive site fingerprinting for analytics/ad pixels, CMS/framework/payment/hosting signatures plus DNS signals (MX/SPF/DMARC), and extraction of published contact evidence (emails, LinkedIn, X, GitHub, telephone links) with page-level source links. Scoring implements the HirePulse gateway v12 method: hiring contributes up to half of the hiring-intent component (highest weight), Google Ads activity contributes up to a mid-range weight based on creative sample counts, and site maturity contributes a smaller capped component based on detected pixels/analytics and technology breadth; the Actor returns numeric scores, signal categories (strong/moderate/weak/none/insufficient_data), reason text tied to specific evidence, section availability/status indicators, sampled ad metadata and counts, hiring metrics and technology/contact inventories.

Use cases

  • Investor screening and venture scouting to prioritize companies showing growth signals
  • Startup research and market diligence using combined hiring, ads, tech and contact evidence
  • Sales prioritization and lead qualification based on hiring intent and advertising activity
  • Building company watchlists and periodic monitoring by comparing repeated runs
  • Enrichment and CRM/automation workflows mapping breakout scores, reasons, hiring counts and public contacts into downstream systems
